This paper speeds up the HQC key encapsulation mechanism by replacing its polynomial multiplication with a Frobenius additive FFT, together with optimized Reed-Solomon encoding and Reed-Muller decoding. The accompanying software covers x86_64 AVX2, x86 with GFNI, Apple M1, and Arm Cortex-A72.[[Paper]](https://doi.org/10.46586/tches.v2026.i2.520-544)[[ePrint]](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/014)[[Code]](https://github.com/ChunTaoPengim/HQC_with_addFFT_tches2026)

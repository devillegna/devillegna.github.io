After the attacks on Rainbow and GeMSS, this paper argues that multivariate cryptography should return to the classical Oil and Vinegar scheme rather than patch the third-round NIST candidates. With appropriate parameters, Oil and Vinegar remains competitive with the new NIST standards on most measures except key size, and the paper revamps the state of the art in implementations for Intel/AMD AVX2, the Arm Cortex-M4, the Xilinx Artix-7 FPGA, and Armv8-A with Neon.[[Paper]](https://doi.org/10.46586/tches.v2023.i3.321-365)[[ePrint]](https://eprint.iacr.org/2023/059)[[Code]](https://github.com/pqov/pqov-paper)

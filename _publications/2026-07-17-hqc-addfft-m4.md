A follow-up to our additive-FFT work on HQC, targeting the Arm Cortex-M4. The paper revisits both the polynomial multiplication and the Reed-Solomon/Reed-Muller encoder and decoder for the microcontroller setting, with results benchmarked on an STM32L4R5 Nucleo-144 board through pqm4.[[Paper]](https://doi.org/10.46586/tches.v2026.i3.377-401)[[ePrint]](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/739)[[Code]](https://github.com/ChunTaoPengim/HQC_with_additive_fft_M4)
